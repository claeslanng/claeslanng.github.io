Guides for the low end

Short, practical answers for 808s, kicks and bass, with the numbers worked out and demos you can hear.

  • Tune your 808 to the keyFind the song's key and the 808's real note, set the root note in FL Studio, and see which notes your 808 can play in any key.
  • BPM to ms calculatorEvery note length in milliseconds for your tempo: straight, dotted and triplet. For delay, reverb and sidechain.
  • Camelot wheelClick a key to see which keys mix with it. All 24 codes, the three safe moves, and the energy-lift trick.
  • Kick and 808 phaseWhy a kick and an 808 can cancel each other out, with a live demo, and four ways to fix it.
  • Bass in monoWhy a wide bass disappears on mono systems and phone speakers, with a demo you can hear.
  • Flow Music plugins on WindowsGoogle's Flow plugins only run on macOS. How to use your .fmx plugins in FL Studio on Windows.
  • 808 note frequency chartEvery note from C0 to B2 in Hz, with FL Studio's octave names and the length of one cycle.

Written by Claes Lanng, who makes the free plugins on this site.
