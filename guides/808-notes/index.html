808 note frequency chart

Most 808s sit in the first octave, roughly C1 (32.7 Hz) to B1 (61.7 Hz). Here's every note from C0 to B2, with the name FL Studio uses for it.

Frequencies use standard tuning, A4 = 440 Hz. "One cycle" is how long one wave of the note takes, which matters when you line a kick up with an 808 (more on that in Kick and 808 phase).

Headphones or a subwoofer help with the listen buttons. Laptop and phone speakers can't play most of these notes.

Why FL Studio shows a different octave

There are two ways to number octaves. Most tuners, PitchScope included, call middle C "C4". FL Studio's piano roll calls it "C5". Same note, same frequency, different name: an 808 that a tuner reads as F#1 is F#2 in FL's piano roll. The chart shows both.

Find the note of your 808

PitchScope reads the note of an 808, bass or kick down to 20 Hz, with the frequency and how many cents off it is. It's free, for Windows, made for FL Studio.
